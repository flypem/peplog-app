## 1. Who we are

## 2. What we collect
- **Account info:** your email address (used only to send sign-in links).
- **App data you enter:** peptide/item names, dose amounts, schedules,
  injection sites, cost figures, and dose-log timestamps.
- **Billing data:** if you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment method
  directly — we never see or store your card number. We keep your Stripe
  customer ID and subscription status.
- **Basic technical data:** standard server logs (IP address, timestamps)
  kept by our hosting provider for security/abuse prevention.

We do **not** collect your name, physical address, or government ID, and
we do not sell your data to anyone.

## 3. How we use it
- To run the app (store and sync your tracked items and dose log)
- To send you the sign-in link you request
- To process your subscription and know which plan you're on
- To fix bugs and understand basic, aggregate usage

## 4. Who else sees it
- **Supabase** (database + authentication hosting)
- **Stripe** (payment processing)
- **[Vercel/your host]** (application hosting)

Each of these processes data on our behalf under their own privacy/security
terms; we don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Your choices
- Export your dose history as a CSV anytime (Pro feature)
- Delete your account and all associated data anytime from Account settings
  (or by emailing us)
- Unsubscribe from the app at any time via the billing portal

## 6. Data retention
We keep your data as long as your account is active, plus [30] days after
deletion to allow recovery of an accidental deletion, then it's permanently
removed.

## 7. Children
Flyptide is not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data
from minors.

## 8. Not medical advice
Data you enter is used only to perform arithmetic you asked for. We are not
a healthcare provider and this policy doesn't change that — see the Terms
of Service for the full disclaimer.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We'll post updates here and adjust the "Last updated" date. Material
changes will be flagged in-app.
