Privacy Policy — Flyptide

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice — have a lawyer review it before relying on it fully.

1. Who we are

Flyptide is a product of Flypem Digital Ventures LLC ("we", "us"), 1309 Coffeen Avenue STE 1200, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801. Contact: contact@flyptide.app.

2. What we collect

Account info: your email address (used only to send sign-in links).
App data you enter: peptide/item names, dose amounts, schedules, injection sites, cost figures, and dose-log timestamps.
Billing data: if you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment method directly — we never see or store your card number. We keep your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.
Basic technical data: standard server logs kept by our hosting provider for security/abuse prevention.

We do not collect your name, physical address, or government ID, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

3. How we use it

To run the app, send you the sign-in link you request, process your subscription, and fix bugs.

4. Who else sees it

Supabase (database + authentication), Stripe (payments), and our hosting provider. We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Your choices

Export your dose history as a CSV anytime (Pro feature). Delete your account and all associated data anytime from Account settings.

6. Data retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion to allow recovery of an accidental deletion, then it's permanently removed.

7. Children

Flyptide is not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Not medical advice

Data you enter is used only to perform arithmetic you asked for. We are not a healthcare provider — see the Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here and adjust the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be flagged in-app.
